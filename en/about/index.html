About this room

Tape Lounge is an independent space connecting crypto prices, trend candidates and public reports. It is not an exchange and this website does not execute real orders. We do not fabricate popularity rankings or visitor counts.

Sources & methodology

Prices: Binance public spot API. Korean quotes: Upbit public API. FX: Frankfurter daily reference. Radar: Andy reports as originally published. Check the source and observation time. Missing APIs or unmatched listings are left blank.

Public observation JSON and RSS are available. reportAt is the observation time, not the page build time. First seen means first within the retained archive, not the first signal in the market. Backtests are not merged into live trading performance.

Privacy & language

Existing anonymous analytics record a random browser ID, screen category, active time and referring domain. We do not collect IPs, fingerprints or search terms. Internal traffic flags persist. Raw sessions are retained for 14 days, then only daily aggregates remain.

For a first-visit language suggestion, country.is receives your request IP. Tape Lounge does not store your IP or location. Browser language is the fallback; your explicit language choice always takes priority.

AI
