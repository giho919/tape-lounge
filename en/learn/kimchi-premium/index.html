Sources and method

Dates are aligned using Bithumb BTC/KRW and USDT/KRW daily closes in Korea time, the Binance BTC/USDT hourly candle ending at midnight Korea time, and Frankfurter daily USD/KRW reference rates. On dates without a published FX rate, the latest earlier rate is carried forward. Only dates with all required data are calculated.

A historical comparison of daily closes across separate markets. It is not an executable quote or an arbitrage opportunity, and does not account for fees, spreads or transfer limits.

Bithumb · Binance · Frankfurter

    01

    The Korean, or kimchi, premium compares a Korean won quote with an overseas quote converted into won. A positive value means the Korean quote is higher; a negative value means it is lower. The result depends on the conversion rate used.

    02

    Using Upbit BTC/KRW, Binance BTC/USDT and a USD/KRW reference, the overseas benchmark is BTC/USDT × USD/KRW. The dollar-based premium is (Korean quote ÷ benchmark − 1) × 100. The USDT/KRW view below reflects local tether pricing and is a separate comparison; sites may define the displayed premium differently.

    03

    For example, if the Korean BTC quote is ₩106,500,000, BTC/USDT is 75,000, USD/KRW is ₩1,400 and USDT/KRW is ₩1,410, the dollar benchmark is ₩105,000,000 and the premium is +1.43%. The tether-based benchmark is ₩105,750,000, leaving the Korean quote +0.71% above it. These are illustrative inputs, not live prices.

    04

    Align quote timestamps. A reference FX rate is not an executable conversion price, and local tether pricing also reflects supply and order-book conditions. Fees, slippage, withdrawal restrictions and transfer time matter, so a negative premium alone does not establish risk-free arbitrage.

    05

    Compare several assets and the USDT/KRW versus USD/KRW gap. That can help separate a coin-specific price difference from broader won or tether demand. See the market page for live domestic and overseas quotes.

    Common questions

    How is the Korean premium calculated?

    Compare BTC/KRW in Korea with BTC/USDT multiplied by a USD/KRW reference rate. This page also shows a separate comparison using local USDT/KRW.

    What does a negative premium mean?

    After conversion on the same basis, the Korean quote is below the overseas benchmark. The figure can vary with venues, FX assumptions and observation times.

    Does a negative premium guarantee arbitrage?

    No. Check executable quotes on both venues, fees, slippage, deposit or withdrawal limits, transfer time and FX before drawing that conclusion.
