Is a negative Korean premium really a bargain?

Separate the exchange rate from the tether basis before reading the gap.

Tape Lounge · 2026-10-22

01

The Korean, or kimchi, premium compares a KRW quote with an overseas quote converted into won. A positive value means the Korean price is higher; a negative value means it is lower.

02

This screen compares Upbit KRW and Binance USDT. Always read both observation times and the FX reference date. A daily reference exchange rate is not an executable, live conversion quote.

03

USDT is not identical to USD. The tether basis, fees, withdrawal restrictions and transfer times affect actual outcomes. A negative premium alone does not establish a risk-free arbitrage.

04

Compare BTC with ETH and the KRW price of tether. A common movement across assets may tell a different story from a short-lived discrepancy at a single exchange.
