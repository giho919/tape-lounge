What should you watch after a breakout?

The level that holds can matter more than the size of the first candle.

Tape Lounge · 2026-10-22

01

One breakout candle does not establish trend persistence. A separate observation is how price closes after returning toward a trend-support level.

02

Support confirmed, awaiting retest and continuation are different observation stages. The same asset can have different states on different timeframes. These are closed-candle assessments at report time, not the current order book.

03

A large distance from support can worsen entry reward-to-risk even if direction is right. Do not confuse the report’s distance with today’s price. Check invalidation levels on the latest chart yourself.

04

Disappearing from the observation list does not prove support failed or a position was sold. The timeline records published reports, not actual trades or an established win rate.
